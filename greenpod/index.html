10-Day Microgreen Calendar Plan
Standard automated timeline for high-yield microgreens cultivation.
Days 1 - 2 · Dark Germination
Closed Canopy + Twice-Daily Air Venting
Lid remains closed in the dark. At 10:00 & 18:30 IST, the lid opens smoothly for a 60-second automated watering, followed by 5 minutes of ventilation before closing.
Day 3 · Canopy Emergence
Permanent Lid Opening + First Light
At 10:00 IST on Day 3, the lid moves smoothly to fully open (180°) and stays open permanently. Grow lights activate and remain on until 23:00 IST.
Days 4 - 9 · Photosynthetic Growth
Automated Irrigation & Full Photoperiod
Full 18-hour photoperiod (05:00 - 23:00 IST). Dual automated waterings at 10:00 & 18:30 IST. Humidity fan activates automatically if tray moisture exceeds 50%.
Day 10 · Harvest Ready
Cycle Complete & Pod Standby
Crop reaches maximum nutritional density and true-leaf emergence. Actuators remain safely managed until harvesting.

Plan Syntax Guide

Each line defines actions for a growth day. The firmware safely schedules actions in order: Lid -> Water -> Fan -> Light -> Close.

D3 LID PERMANENT OPEN 10am
